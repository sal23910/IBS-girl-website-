My Mission to Make Gluten-Free Christmas Party Food

Being gluten-free can be tricky in some situations.

Every Christmas, we used to buy party food from Tesco as part of our food shop. We'd have it on Boxing Day as a treat, and it meant nobody had to cook.

Last Christmas, though, I couldn't have any of my favourite party foods because they all seemed to come with bread, breadcrumbs, or gluten-containing ingredients. I was really sad that I couldn't enjoy my favourites anymore.

When I complained to a manager at my local store, I was told, "Well, we have Plant Chef products you could have."

I replied, "But I'm not vegan — I'm gluten-free."

They're not the same thing, and that's a sale Tesco lost.

I did find a few gluten-free party food options, but there were only about three, and they were all spicy. Spicy food can upset my stomach, so they weren't suitable for me either.

That experience gave me a mission: to make my own gluten-free party food.

So far, I've perfected chicken mini fillets coated in gluten-free breadcrumbs and crushed gluten-free cornflakes, halloumi fries coated in gluten-free breadcrumbs, and mozzarella sticks coated in polenta and gluten-free breadcrumbs.

I've also made delicious breaded mushrooms and Boursin cheese balls using my homemade gluten-free breadcrumbs.

And this week, I've added two more delicious treats to my collection: homemade prawn toast and mini stuffed potatoes with pancetta and cheese.

I'm really enjoying experimenting with different ideas and discovering just how much party food I can make gluten-free.

One of the best things is that many of these homemade treats can be prepared in advance and frozen, ready for when I want them.

My aim is to have a freezer full of homemade gluten-free party food by Christmas, so I can enjoy Boxing Day just like I used to.

Having IBS and eating gluten-free doesn't mean I should have to miss out on all my favourite foods. Sometimes it just means finding a different way to make them.

I'll keep experimenting and sharing what I make on IBS Girl.

I'd love to know what gluten-free party foods other people enjoy making at home.
