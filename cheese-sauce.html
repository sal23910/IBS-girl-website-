Homemade Gluten-Free Cheese Sauce

This is my easy homemade gluten-free cheese sauce. It's perfect for pasta bakes, vegetables and lots of other dishes. I often make extra and freeze it in portions for another day.

Ingredients

  • 25g plain gluten-free flour
  • 25g butter
  • 300ml milk
  • 150g grated cheese
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over a medium heat.
  2. Stir in the gluten-free flour and mix until smooth.
  3. Gradually pour in the milk, stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
  4. Keep stirring until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add the grated cheese and stir until melted and smooth.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

Freezer Tip

Allow the sauce to cool, then portion it into freezer-safe containers and freeze. Defrost in the fridge and reheat gently, stirring until piping hot throughout.
