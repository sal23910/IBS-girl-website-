Making My Own Gluten-Free Stuffed Pasta

22 November 2026

One of the things I really enjoyed before becoming gluten-free was stuffed pasta. There was always such a great range of flavours and fillings to try. But as soon as you become gluten-free, everything changes.

In my local Tesco, the gluten-free stuffed pasta is on the same shelf as the regular pasta. Where there used to be lots of choices, I now only had one to choose from: spinach and basil, which doesn't really do anything for me.

I did go to Sainsbury's and found a spinach and ricotta one, which was nice. I also found prosciutto and ricotta cappelletti in Marks & Spencer, which I enjoyed too. But I still felt like I was being let down when it came to what I could eat.

So I started thinking, could I make my own?

Off I went to have a go!

For my first attempt, I made a minced beef and ricotta filling and then made the pasta dough. It was actually quite quick and easy, although I soon discovered that you have to work quickly because gluten-free pasta dough dries out.

I had to roll it out quite a few times to get it thin enough, but eventually I got there, and it actually looked like a proper sheet of pasta!

I'd bought myself a tortellini cutter, so off I went, making my own stuffed pasta. My ravioli looked OK, and I put it into boiling water, hoping it wouldn't all fall apart.

To my surprise, it stayed together, and it tasted OK too!

In between all this, I'd been talking to a friend who had a pasta machine she was going to give to the charity shop. When she heard what I was doing, she offered it to me instead.

So, here I go again!

This time, I decided to make ham and cheese stuffed pasta, which has always been one of my favourites.

What a difference the pasta machine made! It was so much easier to roll the dough out nice and thin, and the finished pasta tasted really good too.

Another thing I discovered was how easily I could freeze my homemade pasta. I could make a batch, freeze it, and then just take out what I needed.

There's no need to defrost it either. Just pop it straight into boiling water, and a few minutes later, you've got yourself a lovely meal.

I'm really pleased I decided to have a go at making my own gluten-free stuffed pasta. It's something I never thought I'd be doing, but now I can experiment with different fillings and make the flavours I actually enjoy.

Being gluten-free might limit what I can buy in the supermarkets, but it doesn't have to limit what I can make in my own kitchen.
