IBS GIRL WEBSITE

This folder contains your website:
- index.html = the website
- style.css = the design

The site is mobile-friendly and works on Android phones.

IMPORTANT:
1. Replace hello@ibsgirl.example in index.html with your real email address.
2. Replace the sample blog text with your own posts.
3. You can add photos later.

The website is currently a simple one-page blog homepage with sections for:
Home, My Story, Blogs, Recipes and Contact.

You can publish it free using a service such as GitHub Pages or Netlify.

Update: Added Google Analytics virtual page-view tracking for main sections, blog posts and recipes.
