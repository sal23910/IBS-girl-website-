Chewy Gluten-Free Chocolate Chip Cookies

Who doesn't love a homemade chocolate chip cookie? These gluten-free cookies are chewy, full of chocolate chips and perfect with a cup of tea.

Makes: 12 cookies

Preparation: 15 minutes

Chilling: 45 minutes

Baking: 12–13 minutes

Ingredients

  • 230g gluten-free plain flour
  • 130g unsalted butter, softened
  • 150g light brown sugar
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ½ tsp xanthan gum (only if not already in the flour)
  • Pinch of sea salt
  • 130g chocolate chips or chunks

Method

  1. Beat the softened butter, light brown sugar and caster sugar together until light and fluffy.
  2. Add the egg and beat until combined.
  3. Add the gluten-free flour, xanthan gum (if needed), baking powder, bicarbonate of soda and salt. Mix until a dough forms.
  4. Take 100g of the chocolate chips and toss them in a little of the measured gluten-free flour until lightly coated. Fold them into the cookie dough. Keep the remaining 30g for the tops.
  5. Cover the dough and refrigerate for 45 minutes.
  6. Preheat the oven to 170°C fan (180°C conventional). Line baking trays with baking paper.
  7. Divide the chilled dough into 12 balls and place on the baking trays, leaving space between each one.
  8. Press the remaining chocolate chips gently into the tops of the cookies.
  9. Bake for 12–13 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden but the centres are still soft.
  10. Leave the cookies on the baking tray for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool.

My Baking Tip

I like to toss my chocolate chips in a little gluten-free flour before adding them to the mixture. This helps keep them distributed through the mixture rather than sinking to the bottom.

Don't worry if the cookies look slightly soft when they come out of the oven. They will firm up as they cool, leaving a lovely chewy centre.
