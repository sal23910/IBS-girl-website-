Living With IBS – The Side Nobody Sees

1 November 2026

Living with IBS isn't just about having an upset stomach. It's the constant feeling of needing to go to the toilet, sometimes only minutes after you've just been. It's the frustration of thinking you've finished, only to find yourself heading back to the bathroom again. Some days, it feels like your whole life revolves around the toilet.

When you've been to the toilet so many times, all that wiping can leave your bottom feeling sore, raw and sometimes burning. But you have no choice because you keep needing to go again.

And sometimes you just get fed up. Fed up with your stomach controlling your day, fed up with constantly thinking about toilets.

I think one of the hardest things about IBS is that people can't see what you're going through. On the outside, you might look perfectly fine, but inside you're dealing with something that can be physically and mentally exhausting.
