Out With Friends – The Elephant Crossing, Ironbridge

My friend came up from London for a few days and wanted to go out for dinner, so I asked her where she would like to go. She chose a Mexican restaurant called The Elephant Crossing in Ironbridge, which I hadn’t heard of before and isn’t too far from us.

Before we went, I had a look at their website and saw that they offered gluten-free food, so I was really excited to give it a try. When we arrived, I was even more surprised to discover that the whole food menu was gluten-free, apart from some of the beers.

It was such a nice feeling being able to choose whatever I wanted from the menu without having to search for the little GF sign next to every dish or ask which meals could be adapted.

The food we had was lovely, and it made eating out feel so much easier and more relaxing. It’s not often that I can look at a menu and know that almost everything is suitable for me.

I would definitely go back and would happily recommend The Elephant Crossing to anyone looking for somewhere with plenty of gluten-free choices in Ironbridge.
