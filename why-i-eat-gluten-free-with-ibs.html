Why I Eat Gluten-Free Food When I Have IBS

Sunday 8 November 2026

I get asked a lot why I eat gluten-free food when I have IBS, and whether I've been tested for coeliac disease.

Well, the answer is yes. I have had the test, and it came back negative.

However, I found that whenever I ate bread or pasta, my IBS symptoms were really bad, and I spent most of the day going to the toilet.

Once I started eating gluten-free bread and pasta, my whole body felt so much better.

The strange thing is, I can still eat pastry and cake without any problems!

So, you could say I'm half gluten-free. I know it sounds strange, but it works for me, and my body feels so much better for it.

When I eat out at restaurants or go on holiday, I find it much easier just to say I'm gluten-free. That way, I know I'm more likely to be offered food that agrees with me, rather than taking the risk of eating something that could upset my stomach and spoil my day.

Living with IBS is all about finding out what works for your own body, and this is what works for mine.
