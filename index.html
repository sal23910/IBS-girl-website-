GLUTEN-FREE COOKING

My gluten-free recipes

Simple food I enjoy making, with plenty of freezer-friendly ideas for easier days.

Crispy Gluten-Free Chicken Strips

Crispy cornflake-coated chicken strips that can be baked or air fried — and they're freezer friendly too.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast or chicken tenders, cut into strips
  • Gluten-free flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • Certified gluten-free cornflakes, crushed
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • A little cooking oil spray

Method

  1. Put the flour, beaten eggs and crushed cornflakes into three separate shallow bowls. Mix your seasoning into the cornflakes.
  2. Coat each chicken strip in flour, then egg, then press it firmly into the cornflake mixture.
  3. Place on a lined baking tray or in the air fryer basket and lightly spray with oil.
  4. Bake at about 200°C for 15–20 minutes, or air fry at about 200°C for 10–12 minutes, until thoroughly cooked.

Freezer friendly: Allow to cool, freeze, then reheat thoroughly until piping hot throughout.

Crispy Parmesan Leeks

A quick side dish made with buttery leeks, crisp gluten-free breadcrumbs and Parmesan.

Ingredients

  • Leeks, sliced
  • Butter
  • Gluten-free breadcrumbs
  • Parmesan cheese, grated

Method

  1. Melt a little butter in a frying pan and sauté the leeks until soft.
  2. Add the gluten-free breadcrumbs and stir until they become golden and crisp.
  3. Add the grated Parmesan, stir through and serve.

Homemade Gluten-Free Breadcrumbs

A handy way to use up gluten-free bread and keep a supply of breadcrumbs ready in the freezer.

Method

  1. Break the gluten-free bread into chunks and blitz in a food processor until you have breadcrumbs.
  2. Spread the breadcrumbs evenly onto a baking tray and bake for around 20 minutes, or until dry and crispy.
  3. Allow them to cool completely, then place them in a freezer bag and store in the freezer, ready to use whenever you need them.
