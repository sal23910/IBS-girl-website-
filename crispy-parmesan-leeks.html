Crispy Parmesan Leeks with Gluten-Free Breadcrumbs

A quick and easy side dish made with buttery leeks, crispy gluten-free breadcrumbs and Parmesan cheese.

Ingredients

  • Leeks, sliced
  • Butter
  • Gluten-free breadcrumbs
  • Parmesan cheese, grated

Method

  1. Melt a little butter in a frying pan and cook the sliced leeks until softened.
  2. Add the gluten-free breadcrumbs and stir until crispy.
  3. Add the grated Parmesan, stir everything together and serve.
